Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our institutional onboarding and platform capabilities.

How quickly can our institution go live? ▼
Standard provisioning takes under 2 business days. Our automated onboarding engine provisions your dedicated tenant workspace, imports existing faculty/student rosters, and sets up department course mappings instantly.
How does the Clash-Free Timetable Generator work? ▼
Our timetable service runs an automated constraint satisfaction algorithm ensuring that no faculty member or lecture hall is scheduled concurrently across courses, with built-in support for Monday through Saturday schedules and dedicated break intervals.
Is student and finance data strictly isolated? ▼
Yes. All database operations and API requests pass through router-level Tenant Context Middleware with isolated tenant keys and role-based access control, preventing cross-tenant leakage.
Can HODs and Directors contact SaaS support directly from within the app? ▼
Yes. Both the Director and HOD portals feature integrated "Contact SaaS" modals that submit priority support tickets directly to our central SaaS Platform support engineering team.
