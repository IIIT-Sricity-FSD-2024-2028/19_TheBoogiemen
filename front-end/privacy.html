Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to BarelyPassing. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India and applicable educational data privacy norms.

1. Information We Collect

We process data primarily as a Data Fiduciary for our consumer B2C users, and as a Data Processor on behalf of academic institutions (B2B). The data we collect includes:

  • Identity Data: Full name, institutional email address, employee or student ID.
  • Academic Data: Course enrollments, attendance records, assessment marks, submissions, and overall academic performance metrics.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, and system logs utilized strictly for security and diagnostic purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is exclusively used to:

  • Provide and maintain the BarelyPassing platform.
  • Generate academic progress reports and analytics for you and your affiliated institution.
  • Send critical service notifications (e.g., password resets, attendance alerts).
  • Verify institutional affiliations for B2B tenants.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. If you are affiliated with an institution, your academic and identity data is visible to authorized personnel (e.g., Faculty, Academic Heads, Admins) within your institution. We may share data with verified cloud infrastructure providers (e.g., AWS/GCP data centers located strictly within India) to host our platform.

4. Data Localization & Security

All personal and academic data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). In compliance with Indian data sovereignty preferences, primary databases and backups are hosted within the geographical boundaries of India.

5. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data processed by us.
  • Request correction or erasure of your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent (where applicable). However, withdrawing consent for mandatory institutional tracking may require you to contact your institution's administration directly.
  • Register a grievance with our designated Data Protection Officer.
